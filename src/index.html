      Parking lot for thoughts that pop up mid-focus

        Why it works this way
        Procrastination is about feelings, not time
        Putting a task off is a way to escape the bad mood it brings up. That's why each assignment asks how it feels, and "ugh" tasks get the smallest possible first step. Sirois & Pychyl, 2013
        Small, concrete steps beat big vague ones
        A task feels doable when the next action is obvious. Every step here starts with a verb and fits in a short sitting. The first one is a two-minute starter. Steel, 2007
        Planning too much backfires
        People reliably underestimate how long things take. The plan is capped by your energy, and anything past the cap is a bonus. Buehler, Griffin & Ross, 1994
        ADHD brains need the structure outside the head
        Time blindness makes far deadlines feel unreal. The countdown, the visible plan and the timer move the structure onto the page. Barkley, 1997
        Done is better than perfect
        Each assignment has a "good enough" line, so you know when to stop polishing. Perfectionism mostly shows up as delay, not quality.
        Forgive the last slip
        Students who forgave themselves for procrastinating before one exam procrastinated less before the next. Overdue items get a reset, not a scolding. Wohl, Pychyl & Bennett, 2010
        Settings & backup
